Disk Analyzer

Advanced privacy
Clear browsing history and selected advertising-tracker cookies. Saved passwords, ordinary login cookies and bookmarks are preserved. Close the selected browsers before scanning.
This clears local data; it does not spoof your browser fingerprint or delete records held by websites.

    
Performance controls
Pause supported optional background apps and resume them when needed. System processes and NodeMend are protected. Paused apps cannot do background work until resumed.

    
Automatic cache cleaning
Clean safe cache categories while NodeMend stays open. Recycle Bin and personal files are excluded. This does not install a system service or run when NodeMend is closed.
Off by default

    
Synced cloud folders
Analyze a local Google Drive or OneDrive sync folder for large files, then use Duplicate Files to compare contents. This version does not sign into cloud accounts or scan remote-only files through their APIs.
Moving or deleting a synced file can affect other devices and the cloud account. Remote-only files may require their sync client to download data. Direct OAuth cloud cleaning is planned as a separately authorized connection.

    
Proton VPN Free
Provider-hosted VPN with a free individual account and unlimited data. Proton operates the servers; NodeMend does not run a VPN network.
Use your own account and the provider’s official client. Integrated tunnel control is planned, not enabled in this build.
Cloudflare WARP
Free provider-hosted encrypted traffic for everyday network privacy. It does not offer country selection or anonymity.
VPN providers have their own terms and availability. Opening these pages does not install or connect a VPN.
